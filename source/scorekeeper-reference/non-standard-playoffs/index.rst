Non-Standard Playoffs
======================

Overview
--------

In some cases, an event may not have enough teams to fill 8 Alliances for playoffs while also having at least 1 team available to be called in as a backup. This page will go over the process for setting up FMS to handle that and executing the processes during playoffs.

FMS handles this by filling the Alliances that have no real teams with placeholder teams. These are called BYE Alliances. A playoff match that includes a BYE Alliance is a bye match: it is not played, and the other Alliance advances.

Determine number of Alliances
-----------------------------

The recommendation from *FIRST* is that there should be at least one team in the backup pool for the start of playoffs. In order to meet that criteria, an event will need the following number of teams for each desired amount of alliances:

* 4 Alliances: At least 13 teams
* 5 Alliances: At least 16 teams
* 6 Alliances: At least 19 teams
* 7 Alliances: At least 22 teams
* 8 Alliances: At least 25 teams

Do I need to do anything before or during Qualification matches?
----------------------------------------------------------------

No. Set up and run through Qualification matches as you would in every other event. The entirety of this process is after Qualification matches are complete.

After completion of all Qualification matches
---------------------------------------------

Nothing needs to be added by hand. The placeholder teams no longer have to be selected on the Team Selection step; FMS adds them to the event itself when the BYE Alliances are created during Alliance Selection.

During Alliance Selection
-------------------------

Go through the normal :ref:`Alliance Selection <event-wizard-alliance-selection>` process for the Alliances that are made up of "real" teams. Leave the "extra" Alliances completely empty, with no Captain and no selected teams.

* Once the real Alliances are complete, click "Fill Out BYE Alliances" on the Alliance Selection step.
* FMS lists the Alliances it is about to fill and asks for confirmation. Click "Create BYEs" to continue.
* Each Alliance that has no Captain is renamed "BYE - " followed by its Alliance number, and is filled with placeholder Off-Season Demo Teams. A "BYE Alliances Created" message lists the teams placed in each Alliance.
* Finish the Alliance Selection step and build the playoff schedule as you would at any other event.

.. note::
    "Fill Out BYE Alliances" must be used before the playoff schedule is built. FMS will not create BYE Alliances if one of the Alliances without a Captain already has a team selected into it, or if there are not enough unused placeholder teams to fill them.

.. note::
    FMS recognizes a BYE Alliance by its name. Do not rename the BYE Alliances.

During Playoffs
---------------

.. note::
    "Real" teams do not compete in these matches because they are bye matches. Do not have them queue up for these matches!

At an event using the 8 Alliance double elimination bracket, FMS offers to advance the real Alliance automatically when a bye match is the next match to be played:

* When the Match Play tab is opened with the bye match selected, or the bye match becomes the selected match, FMS asks "Advance Non-BYE Alliance?" and states which Alliance advances, for example "Alliance 1 advances over BYE Alliance 8."
* Click "OK" to advance the real Alliance. Click "Cancel" to leave the match unchanged.
* If the first prompt was cancelled, clicking Prestart for the match asks again with a "BYE Match - Choose Action" dialog. Click "Auto-Advance Real Alliance" to advance the real Alliance, or "Continue Prestart" to carry on with a normal Prestart and play the match as described below.

When the real Alliance is advanced automatically, no Prestart is run and no match is played on the field. FMS records the match with all six stations bypassed and a score of 1 to 0 in favor of the real Alliance, commits it with a comment beginning "System-generated BYE result:", posts the result without changing the Audience Display, and moves on to the next match in the schedule.

If both Alliances in a match are BYE Alliances, the BYE Alliance with the lower Alliance number is advanced.

Playing a bye match by hand
---------------------------

If "Continue Prestart" is selected, or FMS does not offer to advance the real Alliance, get through the bye match with a shortened match that nobody plays:

* Before Pre-Starting the match, go to the Options tab in Match Play.
* Change the Teleop Time to 5 seconds and click the Save button below.
* Pre-Start the match and Bypass all teams in the match.
* Once you get the all clear from the FTA, start the match and let it run out.
* Fill in "No" or the equivalent for all teams for auto and endgame (where applicable, season dependent) to fulfill the score validation on Commit.
* Use the "Adjust" field in the bottom right to increase the score by one point for the alliance with the "real" teams so that they win the match.
* Commit the match, then use the "Post Without Display" option in the Post Results dropdown menu.
* If the next match is a "real" match between 6 "real" teams, go back to the Options tab and click the "Restore Defaults" button.

Either way, this process will get through the bye matches quickly and "eliminate" the BYE Alliances after they each lose two matches.
